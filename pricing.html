FAQ · things people actually ask

Real questions, real answers.

What happens when I run out of tokens?

Autonomous jobs pause. Analytics and dashboards keep working. You'll get notified at 20% and again at 5%. Top up manually or enable auto-refill.

Do I get refunded if I undo something?

Yes — anything you undo within 24 hours gets its tokens refunded. After 24h the work has happened, so no refund, but you can still revert.

Can I cap how much Sage spends?

Yes. Set token limits per engine, per week. Set "require approval above X tokens." Pause any engine entirely. You're in control.

What's Beginner vs Advanced mode?

Same engine underneath. Beginner shows outcomes + chat only. Intermediate adds channel breakdown. Advanced exposes every dial.

How is autonomy set?

Per engine, per workspace: Full Auto, Suggest+Confirm, Notify Only, or Off. Defaults match your goal.

Where's my data?

EU and West Africa primary regions. Per-workspace Postgres row-level isolation. AES-256 at rest. NDPR + GDPR compliant.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No annual lock-in unless you opt into the annual 20% discount. Unused tokens stay good for 30 days after cancel.

Do you work with my stack?

WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Squarespace, custom sites (JS SDK), and CI pipelines (GitHub App). If we can't make it work in 5 min, the next call is free.
