FAQ

Things people actually ask.

What happens when I run out of tokens?

Autonomous jobs pause. Analytics and dashboards keep working. You'll get notified at 20% and again at 5%. You can top up manually or enable auto-refill in settings.

Do I get refunded if I undo something?

Yes — anything you undo within 24 hours of execution gets its tokens refunded. After 24h the work has already happened, so no refund, but you can still revert the change.

Can I cap how much SeenFolio can spend?

Yes. Set token limits per engine, per week. Set a "require approval above X tokens" rule. Pause any engine entirely. You're in control.

What's the difference between Beginner, Intermediate, and Advanced mode?

Same engine underneath. Beginner shows outcomes and chat only — no engine names, no technical metrics. Intermediate adds channel breakdowns and preview-before-publish. Advanced exposes every dial, per-engine controls, and the API.

How is autonomy set?

Per engine, per workspace: Full Auto, Suggest+Confirm, Notify Only, or Off. Defaults match your goal — e.g. content gets Suggest+Confirm by default, while small SEO patches go Full Auto.

Where are you hosted? Where's my data?

EU and West Africa primary regions. Data isolation is per-workspace at the Postgres level via row-level security. AES-256 at rest. We're NDPR + GDPR compliant.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No annual lock-in unless you choose the annual plan for the 20% discount. Cancel and your unused tokens stay good for 30 days.

Do you work with my CMS / stack?

WordPress (official plugin), Shopify (Admin app), Webflow, Squarespace, custom sites (JS SDK), and CI pipelines (GitHub App for static sites). If we can't make it work in 5 minutes, the next call is free.
