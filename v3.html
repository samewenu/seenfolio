How is Sage different from automation tools like Zapier?

Zapier executes rules you write. Sage decides what to do, then does it. You don't author triggers — you set a goal ("grow revenue 20% this quarter") and Sage picks the levers each night.

Will Sage actually publish content / ads without me approving?

Only the low-risk, high-confidence stuff — like updating meta descriptions or replying to 5★ reviews. Anything that touches money (ad spend >$50) or your customers' inbox (sends, replies that need nuance) waits in a morning queue with one-tap Approve.

What if Sage does something I don't like?

Every action is reversible for 24 hours with one click — and you get the tokens back. Sage also learns from every undo so the next attempt looks different. We auto-disable any optimization that hurts Core Web Vitals or conversion.

Do I have to ditch my current tools?

Nope. Sage plugs into Google Analytics, Meta Ads, Shopify, Mailchimp, Klaviyo, Stripe, WordPress, WhatsApp Business, and 44 more. She reads from your existing stack — and lets you cancel it when you're ready.

Where does my data live? GDPR / NDPR?

Pick your region at signup (EU-West, US-East, Africa-South1). Single-region by default, no cross-region replication. AES-256-GCM encrypted at rest, RLS-isolated per workspace. Full DPA + sub-processor list on the Trust page.

Can I run Sage in "watch-only" mode at first?

Yes — toggle "Hold all actions" in settings. Sage will still analyze your business and stage recommendations, but won't ship anything until you flip her on. Most customers do this for the first 3 days, then can't help themselves.
