We built the platform we wished existed.

Seenfolio is a tiny team of engineers in Lagos who got tired of duct-taping marketing tools together. So we built one that actually works.

The story

It started with a silver jewelry brand.

In 2025 our co-founder Amara was running a handmade silver jewelry business out of Lagos. She had Google Analytics, Ahrefs, Mailchimp, Meta Ads Manager, and a HubSpot account, and was spending $1,200 a month on tools she barely understood — while still doing all the actual marketing work herself.

We built Seenfolio for her. Then she said "this should be a product." Then she co-founded the company.

Today thousands of online businesses use Seenfolio to replace their marketing stack with one platform that just does the work.

What we believe

Six things we won't compromise on.

Outcomes, not features.

You don't pick between "SEO" and "ads." You pick "more sales." We figure out the rest.

Autonomous, not automated.

Automation = if-then rules. Autonomous = the platform decides what to do. We do the second one.

Trust through transparency.

Every action has a before/after, a reason, and the numbers it moved. No black box.

One-click undo.

Anything we change can be reverted in a single click. And we learn from the undo.

Pay only when we work.

Token-based. Analytics is free. You pay per action we take on your behalf.

Built in Lagos.

Half our customers are in emerging markets. We build for them first, then everyone else.

Wait, why an owl?

Sage watches while you sleep.

"Seenfolio" comes from seen: the act of being noticed. An owl has two enormous eyes — sight made flesh — and works the night shift, which is when most marketing tools quietly fall behind. Sage runs your growth while you're not looking. By morning, you're caught up.
