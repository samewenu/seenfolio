What Sage shipped

Receipts, dated and signed.

Every meaningful release, in chronological order. Subscribe to the feed at changelog.seenfolio.com/feed.xml

May 15, 2026Feature

Workspace switcher v2

Click your workspace name in the topbar to jump between sites instantly. Plus: keyboard shortcut ⌘W. We finally believed our own dogfooding pain on this one.

May 8, 2026Improvement

Sage learned 3 new things

Better at email subject lines, smarter about cart-recovery timing, and now spots GBP photo gaps automatically. Brand-voice training set expanded 4×.

May 1, 2026Integration

TikTok Ads is here

Connect TikTok Ads from Engines → Ads. Variant testing and budget orchestration work out of the box. We've held off on this for six months waiting for the API to stabilize.

April 24, 2026Feature · beta

Crisis Watch (private beta)

Real-time brand mention monitoring with 3σ sentiment alerts. Auto-pauses campaigns when negative sentiment spikes. Email crisis@seenfolio.com for early access.

April 17, 2026Fix

We made errors clearer

Every action now tells you exactly why it ran or didn't — including the model temperature and the prompt fragment. "Sage made a decision" is no longer an acceptable answer.

April 10, 2026Feature

Cloudflare Worker proxy GA

Edge-side rewrites are now available to every workspace. Adds ~6ms to TTFB; doubles GEO score on average. JS SDK still recommended for non-Cloudflare sites.

April 3, 2026Feature

Shapley attribution is now the default

If your workspace has 100+ conversions in the trailing 90 days, we now route to data-driven Shapley attribution instead of time-decay. The old models stay accessible via the toggle.

March 25, 2026Improvement

Sage stopped making up statistics

New factual-claim extractor runs against every generated patch. Any number we can't cite from the source content gets flagged for your review instead of going live.
