06 Notification
Headline change

The trust loop: what / why / result / undo.

The most repeated unit in the product. Every autonomous action collapses to this same four-part shape. Before/after on top, plain reasoning in the middle, hard numbers on the bottom, and a one-click revert that's never hidden.

Visitors arriving from your ads were bouncing because the old headline didn't tell them what you sold. People who can't tell what a site is in 3 seconds leave. The new one names the product and the offer up front.

Plain languageZero jargon. "Bounce rate" is mentioned once, but the explanation is human.
Undo affordanceVisible, single click, includes the learning prompt — not a punishment for the user.

08 Chat
in action

"Write a blog post about silver care."

The chat surface accepts requests, returns a plan, and shows the token cost before work begins. The user is never surprised by their bill. Cheap default actions still need explicit consent if they exceed the user's typical spend pattern.

10 Edge case
Budget exhausted

When the money runs out.

A graceful pause is more reassuring than a panicked alert. The platform stopped spending automatically, explained that the campaign was actually working, and offered three concrete refill paths — not a dropdown.

app.seenfolio.com / alerts / budget-out Triggered 06:14
⏸ Paused · awaiting you

Your ad budget ran out. I paused everything.

Your ads were performing well — $42 to acquire each customer, better than industry average. I stopped them at 6:14 this morning so you wouldn't overspend. Want to keep them running?

Lead with reassurancePerformance was good. Pause was protective, not a failure signal.
Three optionsCognitive load is a feature: a free-form input alone slows decisions.
Auto-refill exitQuietly available — never the default first prompt.
11 Mode toggle
Settings strip

One product, three postures.

90% of users never see this screen. It lives one click deep in settings. The wording — Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced — is honest about what changes; the platform is the same engine underneath, just with more or fewer levers exposed.

Settings · Display mode Switch any time. Your data stays the same.
Default-firstBeginner is pre-selected. The other modes are reached, not stumbled into.
Honest framing"90% of you" — admits the design choice instead of pretending all modes are equal.
