01 — JavaScript SDK

Recommended · 5 min

A single <script> tag. Works on any site — custom builds, Webflow, Squarespace, Wix, anything. Best for non-technical operators who can paste one tag into their site's <head>.

1

Copy your snippet

Find your snippet under Settings → API keys. Each workspace has its own key prefixed sf_live_….

<!-- in <head>, before the closing tag -->
<script src="https://cdn.seenfolio.com/v1/sdk.js"
        data-workspace="sf_live_8h2k…7q1d"
        defer></script>
2

Paste into your site's <head>

Most platforms have a "custom code" or "header tag" setting. We've published copy-paste guides for the top 12 platforms — see Settings → Integration for the path on your platform.

Our SDK is 12.8 KB gzipped and loaded with the defer attribute. It never blocks page render. Performance budget enforced in our CI.
3

Verify in our dashboard

Open Settings → Integration. Status will flip to "Connected" within 30 seconds of the first page view from your site.

If your site sets a strict Content-Security-Policy, you'll need to allow our CDN domain. We auto-detect this and generate the directive for you. See the CSP fix card in your dashboard.

02 — WordPress plugin

5 min · best for WP

Server-side integration via the WP REST API. Lets us modify content directly in your posts and pages with proper meta-field support. Survives theme switches. Recommended for any WordPress site.

1

Install from the WordPress plugin directory

Search "SeenFolio" in Plugins → Add New. Or download the latest .zip:

https://cdn.seenfolio.com/plugins/wp/latest.zip
2

Authorize with your workspace key

In Settings → SeenFolio inside WP, paste your key. We round-trip-verify it against our API.

3

Choose auto-publish or staged-review

Default is staged review: every optimization sits in a 24-hour preview before it goes live. You can flip to auto-publish per page or globally — and undo any change at any time.

We handle both Gutenberg and the Classic Editor. Inline styles are preserved. Custom blocks work via filters.

03 — Cloudflare Worker proxy

10 min · advanced

Edge rewriting. Content is patched at the CDN before the browser ever sees it — best for sites that need perfect server-side SEO and AI engine crawlability. Requires Cloudflare on your domain.

1

Add the SeenFolio Worker as a route

In your Cloudflare dashboard: Workers & Pages → Add Service → Connect to existing Worker. Paste:

// Cloudflare Worker — paste in dashboard or wrangler.toml
name = "seenfolio-edge"
main = "https://workers.seenfolio.com/v1/edge.js"
route = "yourdomain.com/*"
[vars]
SEENFOLIO_WORKSPACE = "sf_live_8h2k…7q1d"
2

Trigger the first content sync

The Worker pulls pre-computed patches from our edge KV. After your first patch is staged, hit the Warm KV button in Settings → Integration.

Worker CPU budget: under 10 ms per request. We never call our origin synchronously. KV consistency is eventual — cache warmup runs automatically post-deploy.

04 — Direct CI pipeline (Git)

15 min · static sites

For Next.js, Astro, SvelteKit, Nuxt, Eleventy, Hugo, Jekyll — anything Git-deployed. We open pull requests against your repo with content patches, run your tests, and trigger your rebuild webhook on merge.

1

Install the SeenFolio GitHub App

Grant write access to the repos you want SeenFolio to optimize. We'll never touch anything outside of MDX, Markdown, JSON content, and SEO meta fields.

https://github.com/apps/seenfolio
2

Add the SeenFolio rebuild secret

If your host uses a deploy webhook (Vercel, Netlify, Cloudflare Pages), paste the webhook URL into Settings → Integration. We trigger it after merge.

SEENFOLIO_REBUILD_WEBHOOK="https://api.vercel.com/v1/integrations/deploy/prj_xxx/abc"
3

Configure your PR style

Choose batch frequency: every optimization, daily, or weekly. Default is weekly batched PRs to avoid review fatigue. Each PR includes the rationale and the SEO/GEO score delta.

If your CI runs Lighthouse on PRs, we use it as a safety net — patches that regress Core Web Vitals get auto-flagged for review instead of merging.

✓

You get a connection email

Within minutes of connection: an email with the first five things we noticed and the first optimization we'd like to make.

Your first seven days

what to expect

A timeline of what SeenFolio does in week one. No two sites are identical, but the cadence is consistent.

D1

Site audit + first recommendations

Full ingest. SEO + GEO + Core Web Vitals scoring on every page. Five highest-impact recommendations surfaced. One small optimization (a meta description fix or two) staged.

D2

Attribution baseline

Your first 24 hours of events build the attribution baseline. By end of day 2 the dashboard shows live revenue, customers, and visitor counts with channel breakdown.

D3

First content patch goes live

The 24-hour staging window on the first optimization elapses. It goes live. Outcome measurement starts (we re-check the affected metric in 7 days).

D5

Content gaps surfaced

We identify high-search-volume queries you don't rank for. Brand voice is calibrated from the existing content we ingested. A draft is ready in your Inbox for review.

D7

First weekly review email

Every Monday at your local 8am: a digest of everything we did the prior week, what worked, what didn't, and what we're planning. You can pause anything in one click.
