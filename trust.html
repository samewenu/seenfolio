Trust & security

Your data lives where you said it should.

Built for businesses in regulated markets (NDPR · GDPR). Region pinning, RLS-isolated tenancy, encrypted credentials, and a public undo guarantee.

Architecture

Three things we got right by default.

Per-tenant data isolation

Postgres Row-Level Security enforces workspace_id on every query. Application-layer enforcement too — defense in depth.

  • RLS policies on every table
  • Per-service DB roles
  • Tested against cross-tenant reads

Encrypted credentials

OAuth tokens for Google/Meta/Shopify and your API keys are AES-256-GCM encrypted at rest. Master key lives outside the database.

  • AES-256-GCM at rest
  • Annual key rotation
  • Refresh rotation on every use

Region pinning

Your data stays in your chosen region (EU-West or US-East). Sub-processors are listed publicly and DPA-bound.

  • Single-region by default
  • No cross-region replication
  • Hetzner-hosted compute + DB

Our promise

The undo guarantee.

100% reversible

Any content patch, ad-budget shift, email send, or autonomous action can be undone within 24 hours. We keep version history for 90 days.

Token refunds on undo

If you undo something within 24 hours, you get those tokens back. We're not in the business of being paid for work you reverted.

Performance auto-protect

If a patch hurts your Core Web Vitals, we automatically disable it and tell you. Speed beats SEO; we know.
